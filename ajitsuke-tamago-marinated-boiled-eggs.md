# Ramen

## Meta

Soft-boiled, marinated eggs for Japanese cooking (Ramen, mostly).

* Servings: 1 per egg
* Calories per serving: see egg packaging, the marinating won't have much effect

## Utensils needed

* Whisk
* Slotted spoon
* Very small, fine needle such as a thumbtack
* Saucepan
* A wide, relatively shallow bowl
* Kitchen roll

## Ingredients

* 6 eggs
* Sake, 120ml
* Soy sauce (japanese, e.g. Kikkoman), 60ml
* Mirin, 60ml
* Sugar, 50g

## Preparation

* Boil a full kettle
* Whisk together the water, sake, soy sauce, mirin, and sugar until dissolved
* For each of the eggs use your thumbtack/needle to poke a small hole at the base of the fat end of the egg
  * This releases the air, preventing air pockets forming

## Cooking

* Pour the boiled kettle water into the saucepan on a high heat
* Once it is boiling, reduce to a bare simmer
* Using the slotted spoon, add each of the eggs one at a time
* Cook for 7 minutes
* Switch off the heat, use the slotted spoon to remove each egg
* Carefully peel eggs under running cold water
* Transfer the eggs to your bowl. Pour marinade in until eggs are just barely covered, or floating
* Place a double layer of kitchen roll over the eggs. Press down until completely saturated in the liquid.
  * This ensures that even if the eggs float, they will be fully submerged in the marinate
* Refrigerate for 4-12 hours. Never longer than 12 hours
* When finished marinating, move the eggs to a container where they will keep in the fridge for 3 days.
