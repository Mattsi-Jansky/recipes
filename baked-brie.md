# Baked Brie

## Meta

A simple appetiser that should keep guests entertained. Dip the crostini, crackers, apple slices, etc in the melted cheese.

* Serves 4

## Utencils needed

* Oven dish
* Parchment paper

## Ingredients

* Full wheel of Brie
* Honey
* Roasted pecans (or substitute with any roasted nut)
* Thyme
* A selection of Crostini and/or crackers
* Red grapes (or other grapes)
* Apples

## Preparation

* Pre-heat the oven to 180C
* Cut the apples into thin but hard slices

## Cooking

* Arrange the parchment paper over the oven dish
* Unwrap the Brie wheel and place it on the parchment paper
* Stick the brie in the oven once it is up to temperature
* Meanwhile, arrange the apple slices, grapes and crostini/crackers on a plate leaving space for the Brie
* Bake the Brie for 15-25 minutes
  * Check whether it is ready by gently prodding the center - it should give way, as if it is about to collpase
* Carefully transfer to the plate (or serve it on the oven dish, warn guests that it is hot)
* Drizzle with honey, and sprinkle with nuts and thyme
