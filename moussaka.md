# Moussaka

## Meta

A tasty oven-cooked, lasagne-like Greek dish.

* Servings: 4

### Utencils needed

* Oven dish
* Saucepan
* Two frying pans

## Ingredients

* red lentils, 160g
* Maris Piper potatoes, 650g
* olive oil, 1tsp
* onions, 2
* garlic cloves, 2
* Thyme, 4 sprigs
* dried oregano, ½ tsp
* ground cinnamon, ½ tsp
* black pepper (preferably freshly ground)
* tomato purée, 1 tbsp
* chopped tomatoes, 400g
* vegetable stock cubes, 1
* sea salt, ½ tsp
* flat leaf parsley, 2 tbsp
* large aubergines, 2
* ricotta, 250g
* mature cheddar, 50g

## Preparation

* Dice or crush the garlic
* Rinse and/or scrub the potatoes if necessary
* Dice the onions
* Boil a full kettle
* Pick the leaves from the 4 thyme sprigs
* Roughly chop the parsley
* Measure out the oregano and cinnamon
* Thinly slice the aubergine
* Pour lentils into a bowl and fill with cold water, set aside to soak

## Cooking

* Bring the potatoes to the boil, then reduce to simmer
* Heat 1tsp olive oil in the frying pan

* 7 MINUTES
