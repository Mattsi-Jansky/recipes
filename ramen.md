# Ramen

## Meta

A vegetarian Ramen with Tonkotsu-style broth.

* Servings: 4-5, can make fewer servings and save remaining broth for another time
* Calories per serving: 700-800 kcal

## Utensils needed

* Large saucepan/pot
* Normal saucepan
* Frying pan
* Ladle
* Blender

## Ingredients

### Broth

* Medium onion, half
* Garlic, 8 cloves
* Ginger, 2.5tbsp
* Peanut oil, 3tbsp
  * Substitute: Any other vegetable cooking oil
* kombu, dried, 8g
* Vegetable stock cubes
  * Consider using low-salt stock cubes, reduces the risk of making the broth too salty
* Cashews, 240ml of
  * TODO: Convert this volume to weight
* Dried shiitake mushroom, 1
* Almond milk, 240ml
  * Or any other plant-based milk without a strong flavour
* toasted sesame oil, 3tbsp
* Soy sauce (japanese, e.g. Kikkoman), 3tbsp
  * Chinese soy sauce has a stronger flavour and will affect the broth
* Nutritional yeast flakes, 120ml

### Ramen

* Fresh ramen noodles
* Selection of mushrooms
  * Shiitake. Can be small, medium, or large. Try a variety
  * Wood ear mushrooms
  * Enoki
  * Try some interesting ones, have some variety
* Bok choy
* Marinated Tofu
* Spring onions, two
* Nori, a few pieces
* Spinach, a few handfuls
* Eggs, room-temperature, one per ramen
  * Optionally use [marinated Ajitsuke Tamago eggs](./ajitsuke-tamago-marinated-boiled-eggs.md)
* Courgette

## Preparation - broth

* Ensure eggs for the later ramen step are not in the fridge, should be at room temperature.
* Mince the garlic cloves
* Halve the onion, store/discard the other half
* Dice the half onion
* Peel and mince the ginger
  * Or: measure out ginger paste
* Prepare 2 litres of vegetable stock
  * Mix 2 litres water with the appropriate number of vegetable stock cubes per the packaging instructions
  * i.e., the package should say specify how many cubes per ml of water
* Measure out the 240ml cashews
* Measure out 8g kombu

## Cooking - broth

* Place shiitake mushroom in 240ml of hot (not boiling) water to rehydrate
* Place cashews in 240ml of hot (not boiling) water to rehydrate
* Heat 3tbsp peanut oil in the large saucepan/pot over medium-high heat
* Once oil is hot add onions, stirring infrequently
* Start timer
* 4 MINUTES
  * Add garlic and ginger
* 6 MINUTES
  * Add vegetable stock
  * Boil and then reduce to simmer
  * Add kombu
* Reset and restart timer
* Meanwhile
  * Measure out nutritional yeast
  * Measure out the almond milk
  * Remove shiitake mushroom and cashews from water, **save the soaking water for later**
  * Blend gently (use a low setting if you have one) the nutritional yeast, sesame oil, soy sauce, the mushroom and cashews, and almond milk for 20 seconds
    * If the consistency is too thick to blend, add some of the water from soaking the mushrooms/cashews
    * Ideal texture should be quite thick but uniform
  * Blend for a further 30 seconds
* 20 MINUTES
  * Take off heat
  * Strain out the onions, garlic, ginger, and kombu
  * Stir in blended cashew/mushroom mix
  * At this point, it should be thick enough to coat a spoon
    * If it is too thick, add some more of the saved water
  * Add 0.3tsp salt, or to taste

## Preparation - Ramen

* Trim and slice the Courgette
* Slice your selection of mushrooms
  * Try to make them roughly similar sizes
  * Small mushrooms can be cooked whole
  * For Enoki mushrooms trim the roots off, pull them apart a little with your hands and throw it all in together. Separate them further with a spoon as they cook.
* Trim the Bok Choy, slice into halves / quite large pieces.
* Measure out as much tofu as you'd like
* Spring onion
  * Trim the ends, branches, and peel the outer-most layer
  * Trim and slice the remaining, store the green and white parts separately

## Cooking - Ramen

* Add a drizzle of oil to your frying pan
* Once hot, add the white spring onion and mushrooms
  * Except for Enoki mushrooms, if using
* stir regularly
* Start timer
* 5 MINUTES
  * Add the Courgette, Tofu and Bok Choy
  * Boil a kettle
* 8 MINUTES
  * If using, add the Enoki mushrooms
* 9 MINUTES
  * Add the green spring onion
* 10 MINUTES
  * Remove from heat
* Put heat under the broth again, ensure it is boiling hot before serving
* Using a saucepan and the boiled water, cook the noodles per packet instructions until ready
* Slice each of the eggs in half vertically
* Serve the noodles, fried vegetables, and eggs between bowls along with a handful of spinach, then ladel as much boiling hot broth as you'd like into each bowl and top with some Nori
