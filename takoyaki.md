# Takoyaki

## Meta

A vegetarian Takoyaki, replacing the oyster with mushroom. Neesd a gas hob, doesn't really work on an electric hob.

## Utensils needed

* Takoyaki pan
* Normal saucepan
* Mixing bowl
* Frying pan
* Saucepan
* Fine collinder/sivve
* Whisk (or big blender/mixer)
* Oil brush
* Wooden skewers or toothpicks

## Ingredients

### Vegetarian Dashi

* 1 piece kombu (approx 10cm square)
* 2tbsp kelp granules
* flax seed oil

### Batter

* 250g plain flour
* 2 large eggs
* 1/tsp Soy sauce (japanese, e.g. Kikkoman)
* 1/2tsp Sea salt

### Fillings, garnish, etc

* vegetable oil
* 1tbsp Butter or margarine
* King oyster mushrooms
* 50g benishouga (red-pickled ginger)
* 50g tenkasu (tempura batter bits)
* 1 large spring onion
* 2tbsp Aonori (aka sea lettuce)
* 1tsp Paprika
* 1tsp garlic powder
* Vegan Okonomi Sauce (also called 'Otafuku Okonomiyaki Sauce Kokusai')
* Mayonaise (preferrably Japanese Mayo, which has a flavour distinct to western mayonnaise)

## Preparation - Dashi and Mushrooms

* Cut the mushrooms into ~1cm cubes

## Cooking - Dashi and Mushrooms

* Make the Dashi
  * Place a litre of cold water into your saucepan and add your kombu piece
  * Bring to a gentle simmer, then remove the kombu
  * Add 2tbsp kelp granules, and three drops of flax seed oil
  * Bring to the boil for 3 seconds, then turn off the heat
  * Allow the pot to stand for 10 minutes
* Cook the fillings
  * Add 1tbsp butter to a frying pan, medium-high heat
  * Once butter is melted, add the chopped mushrooms
  * Sprinkle with 0.5tsp salt, 1tsp paprika, 1tsp gralic powder
  * Cook for 8-10 minutes, until golden brown
  * Strain the dashi to remove the kelp
* Make the batter
  * Can use a mixer/blender here if you have a big enough one
  * Crack 2 large eggs into your large mixing bowl
  * Add and stir in the Dashi
  * Mix in the 250g plain flour
  * Mix in 1/2tsp of your Japanese soy sauce and 1/2tsp of sea salt
  * Mix everything together, outcome should be quite watery and smooth

## Preparation - Takoyaki

* Measure the 50g Tenkasu into a container
* Measure the 50g Benishouga into another container
* Peel spring onion, discard peel, finely slice and separate whites and greens

## Cooking - Takoyaki

* Ensure that all your fillings (Tenkasu, Benishouga, spring onion, cooked mushroom cubes) are arranged into easy to reach places
* Place the Takoyaki pan over your hob and heat until it is hot
* Brush thoroughly with vegetable oil, pour in the batter, completely filling the holes in the pan as well as the surrounds
* Drop a cube of mushroom into each hole
* Scatter whole pan liberally with Tenkasu, Benishouga, and spring onions
* As the batter starts to firm, use your skewer to mark out a grid around each hole, lifting the batter that isn't in the holes
* Insert the skewer into the base of each hole to roll it over and create a sphere
* Remove the balls (toothpicks/skewers help here) and place on a serving tray/plate
* Drizzle liberally with Okonomi sauce, mayonaise, and sprinkle with Aonori
